Put your batch's "past to present" photos in this folder
(images/hero/) — old group photos, ward-day snapshots, past
reunions, right up to your most recent get-together.

Then list them in order in js/main.js, inside the "heroPhotos"
array near the top of the file. Each entry needs a file path
and a short caption (e.g. the year).

Widescreen-ish photos (roughly 3:2) look best in the filmstrip.
If a listed photo file is missing, that frame just shows the
caption on a plain teal background — nothing breaks.
